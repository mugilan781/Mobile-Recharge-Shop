Our Story

From A Small Kiosk To The Neighbourhood's Favourite

RechargeZone began in 2016 as a two-square-metre counter with a single point-of-sale machine and a simple idea: recharge and bill payments should be as easy as buying a newspaper, with prices people can trust.

Today we process over ₹12 crore of transactions every month for 2 million+ customers, run 1,200+ corporate accounts, and still greet every customer by name. The counter got bigger. The promise didn't change.

  • Founded 2016 in City Plaza, New Delhi
  • Authorised agent for 150+ operators & utilities
  • 2M+ customers and counting
  • 24×7 WhatsApp and phone support
