The 2026 Prepaid Plan Cheatsheet: How To Save ₹2,400 A Year

Rohit Arora Aug 5, 2026 8 min read

Every week, someone walks in holding a ₹239 monthly pack and asks if it's good value. The honest answer, most of the time, is no. Here's the ranking we actually use at the counter — and the math behind it.

In this guide
  • What we ranked
  • The cost-per-day method
  • Best plans by tier
  • The recharge-day trick
  • Quick FAQ

What We Actually Ranked

We took the 40 most common prepaid plans across Airtel, Jio and Vi that customers recharge at our counter, then scored them on three things: cost per day of validity, data included per recharge, and network performance in our area (we run speed tests on every operator monthly).

The headline result: annual plans beat monthly plans by roughly ₹2,400 a year for the same daily data allowance. That's a free month and a half of recharge, every year, just for switching billing frequency.

The Cost-Per-Day Method

Plans feel similar until you reduce them to one number: rupees per day of validity. A ₹239 monthly pack is ₹7.97/day. A ₹2,999 annual pack covering 365 days is ₹8.22/day — but it typically bundles daily data you'd otherwise buy as separate top-ups.

Two rules come out of this table. First, never recharge monthly if your operator's quarterly plan exists — the discount is pure savings. Second, always check the plan's data validity; several annual plans expire daily data at 2 AM, so heavy-evening users should pick a plan with rollover.

Counter tip: bring your current plan's SMS or your number to the shop, and we'll show you the exact annual equivalent before you pay. Ten seconds, no pressure.

Best Plans By Tier

Light users (under 2 GB a day)

An annual pack with 1.5 GB daily is enough for messaging, maps and light streaming. Jio's annual value pack and Airtel's 365-day plans were within ₹120 of each other in our June test; pick on network signal, not the sticker price.

Heavy users (2 GB+)

Binge-watchers should skip the popular 1.5 GB plans. The operator "large data" annual packs cost more upfront but remove the panicked mid-month top-up habit — which our logbooks show costs heavy users ₹300–₹500 extra a month.

Number-switchers

If you port providers, never buy an annual plan on the day you switch. Ports take up to 48 hours and the validity clock starts immediately. Recharge monthly for the first cycle, then lock the annual plan once the number settles.

The Recharge-Day Trick

Operators run "recharge day" campaigns a few days each quarter where annual plans ship with bonus data or validity. We see it in our terminal every quarter. Recharging on those days is the single easiest money-saver on this list — same plan, extra weeks.

The catch: the campaign day changes and is announced on short notice. Members of our Gold plan get the schedule on WhatsApp the evening before. Everyone else can just ask at the counter — we track the calendar for you.
