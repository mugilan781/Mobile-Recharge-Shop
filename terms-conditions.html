1. Acceptance

These terms govern every recharge, bill payment and membership at RechargeZone. By requesting any service — in person, by phone, on WhatsApp or via this website — you accept these terms. Please read them once; they're short.

2. Our Services

  • We process prepaid and postpaid mobile recharge, DTH recharge, and electricity, water and gas bill payments through our authorised operator and utility channels.
  • We act as an authorised agent for the providers we support. The operator or utility remains responsible for delivering the recharge or applying the payment.
  • We are not a bank or insurer, and do not hold customer money beyond the wallet balance described below.

3. Payments & Receipts

  • Payment is due at the counter at the time of the transaction. Accepted methods: cash, UPI, debit/credit card and wallet balance.
  • Every transaction receives a printed receipt plus a copy by SMS, WhatsApp or email on request, containing a unique transaction ID.
  • Please verify the number or consumer ID before payment. Recharges made to a number you provided cannot be reversed once delivered.

4. Refunds & Failed Transactions

  • If a transaction fails or is rejected by the provider, we refund the full amount — in cash immediately, or to your wallet instantly.
  • If the provider reports success but the service does not activate, we file a dispute on your behalf and follow it to resolution.
  • Refunds for disputed utility payments depend on the provider's investigation and are governed by their terms.

5. Wallet & Cashback

  • Cashback is credited to your RechargeZone wallet after each eligible transaction.
  • Wallet balance can be used toward any future transaction. It is non-transferable.
  • On request, wallet balance up to the amount not earned as cashback within the last 90 days can be refunded to the original payment method.
  • Cashback rates shown (up to 7%) are indicative; the exact rate depends on provider commission and is confirmed before payment.

6. Memberships

  • Silver, Gold and Platinum memberships are prepaid wallet plans. Bonuses and benefits are described on the Services page.
  • Memberships have no expiry for the balance itself; benefits apply while the plan is active.
  • Membership benefits are personal and non-transferable.

7. Liability

We take care to process every transaction accurately and on time. Our liability is limited to the amount of the specific failed transaction. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, delays caused by operators or utilities, or for information you provide that proves to be incorrect.

8. Contact & Grievances

Questions or grievances about these terms or any transaction: support@rechargezone.in, +91 98765 43210, or in person at Shop #12, City Plaza, New Delhi 110001. We respond to grievances within 5 business days.
